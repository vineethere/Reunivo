Questions people ask

Does the finder need an app?

No. They point their normal camera at the tag and a simple web page opens. If the code is damaged, they can type the short number printed on the tag instead.

Is it a GPS tracker?

No. There's no battery and nothing to charge. A tag works the moment a kind person picks up your thing, and if they allow it, you'll see roughly where it was scanned.

Can someone misuse my tag?

Finders never see your contact details. You can pause a tag at any time, block anyone in the chat, and requests for money are blocked automatically.

Can I move a tag to something else?

Yes. Rename the item or move the tag to something new in a few taps.
