Good questions

Does the finder need an app?+

No. They scan with their normal camera and a simple web page opens. If the QR is damaged, they can type the short code printed on the tag.

Is it a GPS tracker?+

No, and that's the point. No battery, no charging, nothing to break. It works the moment a kind person finds your item, and we show you where it was scanned if they allow location.

Can someone misuse my tag?+

Finders never see your contact details. You can pause or disable a tag any time, block anyone in chat, and money requests are blocked automatically.

Can I move a tag to a different item?+

Yes. Tags are reusable: change what an item is called or move it to something new in a few taps.
